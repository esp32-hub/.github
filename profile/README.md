## About Us

**ESP32 Hub** is a collaborative space for developers, engineers, makers, and technology enthusiasts passionate about embedded systems and connected devices.

Our goal is to build, explore, document, and share projects powered by the ESP32 ecosystem.

We believe in learning through experimentation, sharing knowledge, and making technology more accessible through open-source collaboration.

## What We Do

- **Embedded Systems** — Firmware development, microcontrollers, and low-level programming.
- **Internet of Things (IoT)** — Connected devices, sensors, automation, and communication protocols.
- **Electronics & Hardware** — Circuit design, PCB development, and hardware experimentation.
- **Software & Integration** — APIs, backend services, device communication, and system architectures.
- **Open Source** — Sharing projects, documentation, tools, and knowledge with the community.

## Our Technologies

We work with technologies and tools across the embedded development ecosystem:

`ESP32` · `ESP-IDF` · `Arduino` · `C` · `C++` · `MicroPython` · `FreeRTOS` · `PlatformIO` · `MQTT` · `Wi-Fi` · `Bluetooth LE`

## Our Projects

We aim to create practical, well-documented projects that encourage experimentation and collaboration.

Explore our repositories to discover firmware, hardware integrations, tools, experiments, and complete IoT solutions.

## Get Involved

Open source grows through collaboration.

Whether you're an experienced embedded developer or just getting started with microcontrollers, you're welcome to explore our projects, report issues, suggest improvements, and contribute.

## Our Philosophy

**Build openly. Learn continuously. Share knowledge.**
